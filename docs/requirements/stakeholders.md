# Stakeholders

## Project Title

**HostelFix — Smart Hostel Complaint & Mess Management System**

---

## Overview

A stakeholder is any individual, group, or entity that is affected by or has an interest in the HostelFix system. The following stakeholders have been identified for this project.

---

## Primary Stakeholders

Primary stakeholders directly interact with the HostelFix system through a defined user role.

---

### 1. Student

**Description:**
Students are the primary end-users of the HostelFix system. They reside in the hostel and depend on hostel administration to address maintenance, infrastructure, and service-related issues.

**Interaction with the System:**
- Register an account and log in to the system
- Raise new complaints by selecting a category, providing a description, and optionally attaching evidence
- View all complaints they have submitted
- Track the current status of each complaint
- View the complete status history (timeline) of each complaint
- View the weekly mess menu published by the warden
- Submit feedback and ratings on mess meals and services

**Pain Points Addressed:**
- No longer have to depend on informal channels to submit complaints
- Can track complaint progress without needing to contact the warden directly
- Have access to organized mess information in one place
- Can provide structured feedback that is actually recorded

**Primary Goal:** Submit complaints easily, monitor their resolution, and access hostel services information in one place.

---

### 2. Warden

**Description:**
The warden is the administrative authority responsible for managing the hostel. The warden oversees complaint resolution, manages hostel staff, and administers mess-related services.

**Interaction with the System:**
- Log in to the system with warden-level access
- View all complaints submitted by students across the hostel
- Review complaint details and decide to approve or reject each complaint
- Assign approved complaints to specific staff members
- Monitor the status of all complaints in progress
- Close complaints that have been resolved by staff
- Create and update the weekly mess menu
- View summarized mess feedback submitted by students
- Access a dashboard with a current overview of complaint status

**Pain Points Addressed:**
- No longer needs to manage complaints through informal channels
- Has a centralized view of all pending, active, and resolved complaints
- Can formally assign staff to complaints and track resolution
- Can manage mess information and review student feedback in one place

**Primary Goal:** Efficiently manage the full hostel complaint lifecycle and administer mess-related services with visibility and control.

---

### 3. Hostel Staff

**Description:**
Hostel staff are maintenance or service personnel responsible for resolving complaints assigned to them by the warden. They may include electricians, plumbers, cleaners, carpenters, and other maintenance workers.

**Interaction with the System:**
- Log in to the system with staff-level access
- View the list of complaints assigned to them by the warden
- View full details of each assigned complaint
- Update the status of assigned complaints as work progresses (e.g., In Progress)
- Mark assigned complaints as resolved upon completion of work

**Pain Points Addressed:**
- Receive task assignments through a formal system rather than verbal instructions
- Have clear complaint details including category, description, and any attached evidence
- Can formally record resolution, creating an accountable record

**Primary Goal:** Receive, work on, and resolve assigned complaints with clear task information and status update capability.

---

## Secondary Stakeholders

Secondary stakeholders benefit from the system but do not directly interact with it through a login role.

---

### 4. Hostel Administration

**Description:**
The hostel administration includes the college administration, hostel management committee, or principal's office that oversees hostel operations at an institutional level.

**Benefits from the System:**
- Access to centralized complaint records that support institutional oversight
- Improved accountability through a traceable complaint lifecycle
- Better monitoring of recurring hostel infrastructure issues
- Evidence of organized and systematic hostel operations during academic evaluations or inspections
- Foundation for future reporting and analytics capabilities

**Primary Benefit:** Improved organizational oversight, accountability, and institutional credibility in hostel management.

---

## Stakeholder Summary Table

| Stakeholder | Type | Interacts Directly | Primary Concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | Primary | Yes (Student role) | Submit and track complaints; access mess information |
| Warden | Primary | Yes (Warden role) | Manage complaints; administer mess; oversee resolution |
| Hostel Staff | Primary | Yes (Staff role) | View and resolve assigned complaints |
| Hostel Administration | Secondary | No | Oversight, accountability, and institutional records |

---

## Notes

- The three direct-interaction stakeholders correspond exactly to the three system roles: **Student**, **Warden**, and **Staff**.
- Role permissions for each stakeholder are defined in detail in the User Roles document.
- Hostel Administration has been identified as a secondary stakeholder. Future phases may introduce administrative reporting or read-only access for this group.
