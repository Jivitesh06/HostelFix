# Project Objectives

## Project Title

**HostelFix — Smart Hostel Complaint & Mess Management System**

---

## Overview

The following objectives define what HostelFix is intended to achieve. Each objective is directly connected to the problems identified in the problem statement and corresponds to a functional area of the system.

---

## Objectives

### OBJ-01 — Digitize Hostel Complaint Registration

Replace informal complaint submission methods (verbal communication, WhatsApp, physical registers) with a structured digital complaint submission interface accessible to all students.

### OBJ-02 — Provide a Structured Complaint Submission Interface for Students

Allow students to raise complaints by specifying a complaint category, providing a description, and optionally attaching supporting evidence such as images. This ensures complaints are complete and actionable from the point of submission.

### OBJ-03 — Allow Students to Track Complaint Status

Provide students with real-time visibility into the current status of their submitted complaints. Students should be able to see whether a complaint is pending, approved, assigned, in progress, resolved, or closed.

### OBJ-04 — Allow Wardens to Review and Manage Complaints

Give wardens a centralized view of all submitted complaints. Wardens should be able to review complaint details and make informed decisions to approve or reject each complaint.

### OBJ-05 — Allow Wardens to Assign Complaints to Appropriate Staff

Enable wardens to assign approved complaints to specific staff members. This formalizes responsibility and ensures that each complaint has a designated person accountable for resolution.

### OBJ-06 — Allow Staff to Manage Their Assigned Complaints

Provide staff members with a task-focused view of complaints assigned to them. Staff should be able to view complaint details, update the complaint status as work progresses, and mark a complaint as resolved upon completion.

### OBJ-07 — Maintain Complaint Status History

Record every status transition a complaint undergoes throughout its lifecycle. This history provides an audit trail that supports accountability, transparency, and dispute resolution between students, staff, and wardens.

### OBJ-08 — Provide Centralized Weekly Mess Menu Information

Allow wardens to publish and update the weekly mess menu within the system. Students should be able to view the current mess menu at any time without relying on notice boards or informal communication channels.

### OBJ-09 — Allow Students to Provide Mess Feedback

Enable students to submit ratings and written feedback on mess meals and services. This provides the warden with structured input to evaluate and improve mess quality.

### OBJ-10 — Improve Accountability and Transparency in Hostel Complaint Management

Through the complaint lifecycle, assignment records, status history, and role-based dashboards, ensure that all stakeholders — students, staff, wardens, and administration — have appropriate visibility and accountability for hostel complaint resolution.

---

## Relationship Between Objectives and Problems

| Objective | Problem Addressed |
|-----------|-------------------|
| OBJ-01 | Complaints submitted through informal channels; no permanent record |
| OBJ-02 | Incomplete complaints; lack of actionable information at submission |
| OBJ-03 | Students have no visibility into complaint progress after submission |
| OBJ-04 | No centralized view for wardens; complaints go unreviewed |
| OBJ-05 | No formal assignment of responsibility to specific staff |
| OBJ-06 | Staff have no structured way to receive and manage assigned tasks |
| OBJ-07 | No audit trail; recurring issues and accountability cannot be tracked |
| OBJ-08 | Mess menu is communicated informally through notice boards or messaging apps |
| OBJ-09 | No structured feedback mechanism for mess quality |
| OBJ-10 | Lack of accountability and transparency across the hostel management chain |

---

## Notes

- All objectives are directly connected to the system scope defined for HostelFix.
- Objectives that are **not** in scope for the current phase (such as automated SLA monitoring, notifications, and analytics) are documented in the System Scope document and classified as future scope.
