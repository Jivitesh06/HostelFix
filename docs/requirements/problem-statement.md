# Problem Statement

## Project Title

**HostelFix — Smart Hostel Complaint & Mess Management System**

---

## Background

College hostels accommodate a large number of students who depend on hostel administration for maintenance, cleanliness, infrastructure, and daily services such as mess meals. Effective communication between students and hostel administration is critical for a functional and safe living environment.

In the current state, hostel complaint management at most institutions relies on informal and unstructured channels including:

- Verbal communication with wardens or hostel office staff
- WhatsApp messages or phone calls to wardens
- Physical complaint registers maintained at the hostel office
- Manual note-passing or letter submissions

Similarly, mess-related information such as weekly menus and student feedback is shared informally through notice boards, WhatsApp groups, or word of mouth.

---

## Problem Description

The absence of a centralized and structured system for hostel complaint and mess management creates the following problems:

### 1. Complaints Are Lost or Forgotten

When complaints are submitted verbally or over messaging apps, there is no permanent record. Complaints can be missed, forgotten, or deprioritized without any notification to the student.

### 2. No Centralized Tracking

There is no single place where all complaints are recorded. Wardens, staff, and students must rely on memory or informal notes to track the status of a complaint, making monitoring unreliable.

### 3. Unclear Responsibility

When a complaint is reported informally, there is no formal assignment to a specific staff member. This creates confusion about who is responsible for resolution and allows complaints to remain unattended.

### 4. Lack of Transparency for Students

Students have no visibility into what is happening with their complaint after submission. They cannot determine whether their complaint has been received, is being worked on, or has been rejected.

### 5. Difficulty Monitoring Resolution

Wardens and hostel administration cannot efficiently monitor which complaints are pending, in progress, or overdue. Without structured records, systematic accountability is impossible.

### 6. Recurring Issues Go Unnoticed

Without complaint records, recurring infrastructure or maintenance problems cannot be identified easily. The same issue may be reported multiple times without being recognized as a pattern.

### 7. No Organized Mess Information

There is no dedicated platform for students to view the weekly mess menu in advance. Students often do not know what meals are scheduled, which affects planning and satisfaction.

### 8. No Structured Mess Feedback

Students cannot provide structured feedback on mess quality and meals. Wardens cannot systematically collect or review mess-related feedback to improve services.

---

## Proposed Solution

**HostelFix** is a centralized web-based hostel complaint and mess management system that addresses these problems by:

- Providing a structured complaint submission interface for students
- Maintaining a complete and permanent complaint record with lifecycle tracking
- Allowing wardens to review, approve, reject, and assign complaints
- Allowing staff to view and manage their assigned complaints
- Maintaining a full status history for every complaint
- Providing a centralized mess menu managed by the warden
- Allowing students to submit structured mess feedback

---

## Problem Scope

This problem statement applies to:

- **Students** residing in college hostels who require a reliable channel to raise and track complaints
- **Wardens** responsible for managing hostel operations and ensuring complaint resolution
- **Hostel Staff** who perform the actual maintenance and resolution work
- **Hostel Administration** that requires oversight and accountability for hostel operations

---

## Summary

> The core problem is the absence of a centralized, structured, and transparent system for managing hostel complaints and mess information. HostelFix is proposed as a practical solution that digitizes the complaint lifecycle, assigns accountability, and provides visibility to all stakeholders involved in hostel management.
